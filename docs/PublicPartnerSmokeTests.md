# Public Partner Smoke Tests

Use development data or temporary UI fixtures with zero, one and multiple linked partners, unknown birthdays, a deceased partner and duplicate names. Keep database, migration and seed files unchanged. Include a partner whose own id differs from its associated Cherry Kin id.

- In Genealogy, verify the shaded root group has no heading for zero, one and multiple partners. All linked partners appear directly below the root, including partners without birthdays. A sole partner's name aligns with the root name; with two or more partners, all partner names are slightly indented.
- Verify missing partner birthdays leave Age and Birthed blank. Dated partners use the public full-month date format. Partner Spouse cells show the associated Cherry Kin Full Name; Cherry Kin Spouse cells show all partner names separated by commas.
- Verify one subtle box encloses the root and all linked partners, including a root without partners. Click the root name: that exact Cherry Kin remains selected. Partner names are plain text, have no pointer/selection hover styling, and do not receive keyboard focus or change the root when clicked.
- Verify column headings appear inside the shaded root box above its rows, including when ancestry appears above the box. Ancestry and descendant tables have no visible column headings but retain them for screen readers. List column headings remain visible.
- Select a Cherry Kin with at least four ancestors. Above the boxed group, verify exactly Great Grandparents, Grandparents and Parents in that top-down order, each with the Cherry Kin reached by the next parent_id and all of that ancestor's partners. Descendant sections remain below the boxed group. Verify zero, one and multiple partners, unknown birthdays, duplicate names, and overlapping people/partner IDs. No spouse ancestry contributes a branch. Click each ancestor name and verify that exact Cherry Kin becomes the root, updating Spouses, ancestors and descendants together.
- Check roots with zero, one and two ancestors: only existing ancestor headings appear, and no later heading appears after a NULL parent. A top-level root has none. Check ancestor search highlighting, partner indentation, Age details and Cherry Kin phone controls at desktop and mobile widths.
- Check all four descendant sections still contain only Cherry Kin with their original counts and order. A descendant's partners appear as rows only after that descendant becomes the root.
- In List, verify one row per Cherry Kin and one row per partner, including partners without birthdays. With Search empty, the row count must equal people plus partners for every sort except Date of Passing, which omits all entries with a NULL Death Day. Partners without birthdays have blank Age and Birthed cells. Duplicate names remain separate rows, including multiple partners associated with the same Cherry Kin.
- Select Date of Passing and verify the columns are Name, Age, Birthed, Passed Away, Children, Spouse. Passed Away uses each row's own Death Day in the same format as Birthed (e.g. March 14, 2008), ordered earliest to latest including the year. Switch to another sort or Genealogy and confirm Passed Away disappears; return to List with Date of Passing remembered and confirm it reappears. Search for no matches and confirm the empty message spans all six columns.
- Click a List partner name and verify the List and root selection do not change. Click a Cherry Kin name and verify that exact Cherry Kin becomes the genealogy root, including when names are duplicated. Check keyboard activation, retained Search, blank Genealogy Sort and restored List Sort when toggling back.
- Verify a sole partner displays the associated Cherry Kin's Children count, including 0, whether or not Birthday is known. Multiple partners linked to the same Cherry Kin must all have blank Children, even when Search only shows one of them. Children sorts numeric counts descending with blanks after 0. Test all existing sorts: Date of Passing omits Cherry Kin and partners with NULL Death Days; missing Birthdays and Spouses stay visible at the bottom of their respective sorts. Missing birthdays are not highlighted by Upcoming Birthday. Check preserved List sort when switching views. No Age sort or direction control is added.
- Search for partial partner names and associated Cherry Kin names with mixed case and surrounding spaces. Match Full Name OR Spouse only; dates and ages must not match. Genealogy highlights matches and List filters them. Check empty results and Reset.
- Compare a deceased partner's age, dagger, accessible label and popup with Cherry Kin behavior. Check partner birthday/deathday supply the age at death, death date and hypothetical age today. Test hover, focus, click, Enter, Escape and outside dismissal, plus touch activation at a mobile width.
- Verify partners never have phone icons. Genealogy Cherry Kin retain phone details; List contains no phone controls. Check indentation and popup positioning at desktop and mobile widths.
- Confirm the public server read selects no legacy spouse columns from people, and the server-to-client data contains independent people and partners records. Check the unchanged admin list still loads.
